A group of independent companies · Tallinn, Estonia

Independent companies along the Estonia–Latin America corridor.

LedeLab Group OÜ is an Estonian parent that holds a common stake in a set of independent companies — across health, education, sustainable materials and media. Each is run on its own; what binds them is a single thesis: bridging Estonia’s digital-nation infrastructure with the talent and markets of Latin America.

01 The thesis → the spine of the whole group

Why Estonia ↔ Latin America.

Estonia pioneered the digital state — e-Residency, e-government, a clean gateway into the EU. Latin America has the talent, the markets and the urgency. Few operators sit credibly at both ends. We do: Estonian infrastructure as the backbone, Latin American roots as the engine.
